# Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance, customer behavior, and profitability.

## Project Overview

This project explores sales data to identify key business insights related to revenue, profit, customers, products, categories, regions, and discounts.

The goal of the dashboard is to provide a clear overview of business performance and help understand which areas generate the most revenue and profit.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data visualization

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance Overview
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Revenue trend over time
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by region

### 2. Interactive Analysis
- Interactive filters by year, category, and region
- Top products by revenue
- Top customers by revenue
- Revenue trend analysis
- Regional and category breakdowns

### 3. Customer Insights
- Top customers by revenue
- Top customers by profit
- Total customers
- Average revenue per customer

### 4. Profit Analysis
- Profit by category
- Profit margin by category
- Discount impact on average profit

## Key Insights

- Top revenue customers are not always the most profitable customers.
- Some categories generate strong revenue but have lower profit margins.
- Higher discounts are associated with lower average profit.
- Regional and category-level filtering helps identify performance patterns.
